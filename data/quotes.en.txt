Snow is coming tonight. Shall we have one more cup?
Where the water ends, I sit and watch the clouds rise.
Picking chrysanthemums by the eastern fence, the hills appear.
Quiet mountain, falling blossoms, an empty evening.
The pine wind loosens my belt; the moon lights the zither.
Fine rain, the fish rise; a soft breeze, the swallows tilt.
Let the evening come slowly. Nothing here needs doing.
The moon does not hurry, and neither does the tide.
